Illustrative brief / LinkedIn post for a B2B software brand

Your product has 12 features.
Your buyer has one problem.

A product page can explain every feature and still leave a buyer asking, “Will this help my team?”

Start with the moment they recognise: the missed handover, the report rebuilt every Friday, the same information entered twice.

Then connect one relevant feature to that problem. Show what changes in their working day. Give them a concrete next step.

The useful question before publishing: have we explained the product, or helped someone make a decision?

Why this approach: A recognisable problem, a clear benefit and a question that invites a useful conversation.

Illustrative brief / Nurture after a B2B content download

Give the next email a reason to exist.

Email 1 / Deliver the resourceSubject: Your campaign planning checklist is here

Here’s the checklist you requested. Start with the audience and goal sections, then use the channel planner to connect each activity to one outcome.

CTA: Open your checklist

Email 2 / Help them use itSubject: One question before you choose your channels

What should someone do after seeing your campaign? Choose that action first. It makes the message, channel and measurement decisions easier.

CTA: See a worked example

Email 3 / Offer a relevant next stepSubject: Want a second pair of eyes on your plan?

If you’re putting the checklist into practice, bring your biggest planning question to a short walkthrough. We’ll focus on the step that’s slowing you down.

CTA: Book a walkthrough

Measurement plan: Track resource clicks, replies and walkthrough bookings. Use email opens as a directional signal. Respect consent and stop or adapt the sequence when someone converts.

Illustrative brief / Streaming campaign delivery checklist

A smooth launch starts before “publish”.

  • 01
    Confirm the brief.
    Audience, title, channel, market, delivery owner and deadline.
  • 02
    Check the asset.
    Correct version, format, captions, credits and approved copy.
  • 03
    Validate publishing details.
    Metadata, links, placement and the agreed publishing window.
  • 04
    Verify after publishing.
    Check the live content, log issues and confirm the handover.
  • 05
    Close the loop.
    Review performance and record one improvement for the next delivery.

Why this approach: Clear owners and repeatable checks reduce avoidable rework without adding unnecessary process.
