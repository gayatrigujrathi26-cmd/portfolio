Building a CEO's Public Voice

Social Media & Content Executive · Mumbai
Aug 2023 – Sep 2024

Sole point of contact for 20+ B2B accounts across pharma, healthcare, consulting and the public sector, including ghostwriting the public voice of a pharma and eyecare CEO.

The situation

She was put in as the single point of contact for 20+ B2B accounts. The toughest of them: a pharma and eyecare company's CEO who needed a credible public voice in an industry where the wrong claim is a compliance problem, not just a bad look.

The approach

Built his thought leadership profile from the ground up: insightful industry content and engaging infographics, run on a consistent posting schedule with deliberate networking rather than one-off posts. The same discipline (accurate in a regulated space, still written for a human) carried across the rest of the roster, backed by HubSpot, Mailchimp and Brevo nurture sequences A/B tested until open rates moved, GA4 and LinkedIn Analytics to keep every account's content targeted, and 15+ multi-format assets shipped a week to keep it all running.

The result

Profile visibility up 40% with higher follower interaction for the CEO specifically. 35% average ROI lift and 18% higher email open rates across the account base. All 20+ accounts retained.

Making Products Findable, and Sellable

Marketing Communications Executive · Mumbai
Jul 2022 – Jul 2023

Go-to-market and content for an edtech business that needed its products found twice over: through search, and through its own Amazon listings.

The situation

The products needed to be found organically and sold directly on Amazon Marketplace, where the listing itself had to do the selling with no salesperson in between.

The approach

Built an SEO content strategy on Google Ads, SEMrush and HubSpot, researching and targeting keywords that actually converted rather than just ranked. Rebuilt the Amazon Marketplace listings and A+ content into accurate, conversion-ready product pages. Backed both with 50+ B2B go-to-market launches across web, email, social and events, and 12+ trade events and conferences a year to keep demand flowing into those pages.

The result

Organic traffic up 20%, product adoption up 12%, 95% satisfaction across 12+ annual events, and a set of Amazon listings that finally matched the product.

FILE 03 / 04

Running Content at IPL Scale

Digital Media & Content Curation Executive · Mumbai
May 2021 – Apr 2022

Content and campaign operations at national scale, IPL season included, for one of India's largest streaming platforms.

The situation

During IPL season, the platform needed 300+ campaign assets live across four channels at once, with no room for a bottleneck between an asset being ready and it going out.

The approach

Owned the CMS pipeline end to end: coordinated 10 deliverables a day across YouTube, Instagram, Facebook and X, and built real-time performance dashboards in Google Data Studio and GA4 to see what was landing before the next asset shipped. When peak periods (IPL, festivals, blockbuster releases) started to strain the workflow, rebuilt the delivery and reporting process itself rather than just pushing through it.

The result

300+ assets managed through the CMS, dashboard responsiveness up 25%, turnaround time down 15% during peak periods.
